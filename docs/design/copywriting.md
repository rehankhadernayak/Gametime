## FEATURES SECTION

### `<h2>` — Section Headline
**"Built for how your family actually plays."**

---

### Feature Block 1: Dual-Points Economy

**`<h3>` Headline:**
"Two currencies. One family system."

**`<p>` Body:**
Gametime runs on two types of points. Regular Points (RP) are earned through tasks and spent on custom rewards you create — extra screen time, a movie night, anything you decide. Giftcard Points (GP) are funded by you — real money, converted into digital credits your child can spend on actual gift cards. Both balances are visible to everyone. No surprises. No "but you promised."

**`<p class="caption">` Feature callout:**
Parents set the RP value per task (5–50 points). GP funding is parent-controlled. Every transaction is logged.

---

### Feature Block 2: Evidence-Based Completion (No Self-Reporting, Claude Vision AI)

**`<h3>` Headline:**
"Your child can't just say they did it."

**`<p>` Body:**
Task completion requires a photo or short video submitted through the app. Our built-in AI — powered by Claude Vision — reviews the evidence before it reaches you, scoring it as Approve, Needs Review, or Return. You see the AI's reasoning alongside the evidence. You make the final call. It takes about 10 seconds.

**`<p class="caption">` Feature callout:**
AI review is non-blocking — tasks never get stuck waiting. If the API is unavailable, a local review engine steps in automatically.

---

### Feature Block 3: Gaming-Native Rewards

**`<h3>` Headline:**
"The rewards they actually want to work for."

**`<p>` Body:**
Gametime integrates with the digital gift cards your child already cares about: Roblox Robux, Steam Wallet, Razer Gold, Xbox, PlayStation, Minecraft, Fortnite, Nintendo, Google Play, and Apple. Not vouchers you have to go buy. Not points toward a stuffed toy. Real codes, delivered in-app, earned through real work.

**`<p class="caption">` Feature callout:**
Gift card codes are encrypted in storage and only revealed once a parent marks the redemption fulfilled.

## FAQ SECTION

### `<h2>` — Headline
**"Questions before you commit."**

---

### `<details>` Q1
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** What is Gametime?

**`<p>`** Gametime is a family app that connects household responsibilities — homework, chores, goals — to the digital rewards children actually want: Roblox credits, Steam wallet funds, gaming time. Parents create tasks, children submit photo or video proof, AI assists with review, and parents approve. Approved tasks earn points that can be redeemed for real gift cards or gaming time extensions.

---

### `<details>` Q2
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** How do Giftcard Points work?

**`<p>`** Giftcard Points (GP) are funded directly by parents — think of it as a digital allowance. You top up via Stripe, the funds are converted to GP and allocated to your children's accounts. When a child redeems a GP reward, you fulfill it by delivering the gift card code through the app. The code is encrypted until the moment of reveal.

---

### `<details>` Q3
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** Is my child's data safe?

**`<p>`** Gametime is built and operated in compliance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). We collect only what's needed to run the app — no advertising data, no third-party data sharing. Evidence photos and videos are stored securely, linked only to the task completion record, and never used for any purpose other than the parent review. Gift card codes are encrypted using AES-256-GCM and are never stored in plain text.

---

### `<details>` Q4
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** What age is it suitable for?

**`<p>`** Gametime is designed for children aged 6–13. Younger children (6–9) use a simple 4-digit PIN login on a large, friendly interface with no reading required for core functions. Older children (10–13) can have a full email/password login and access more detailed stats and reward browsing. Parents manage all account settings.

---

### `<details>` Q5
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** What gaming platforms and gift cards are supported?

**`<p>`** Currently supported: Roblox, Steam, Razer Gold, Xbox Gift Card, PlayStation Store, Valorant, Nintendo eShop, Fortnite, Minecraft, Google Play, and Apple. We add new providers as they become available through our integration partner. Physical gaming time controls work independently of gift cards — you can control session length on any device category regardless of which gift cards you use.

---

### `<details>` Q6
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** Can I set different rules for different children?

**`<p>`** Yes. Each child has a completely independent profile with their own point balance, task assignments, gaming session limits, and reward redemption history. A rule for your 8-year-old doesn't affect your 12-year-old. You can also assign different tasks with different point values to different children at the same time.

---

### `<details>` Q7
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** How much does it cost during beta?

**`<p>`** Beta access is completely free. You only spend real money if you choose to fund Giftcard Points — that's your money, going directly toward real gift cards for your children. There is no platform fee, no subscription, and no in-app purchase that doesn't go directly to your child's reward balance.

---

### `<details>` Q8
**`<summary>` `<h3>`** What happens after beta?

**`<p>`** We'll introduce a subscription plan for full platform access. Beta families will get a founding member pricing tier — locked in before we announce public pricing. We'll give you at least 30 days notice before any billing begins, and you'll always be able to export your family's data.
